TOUCH / ANDROID CONTROLS

  • Auto-Drive: On by default! Craft accelerates automatically so you can focus on steering and high-speed banking.
  • Left Thumb: Tap or drag left/right on the steering pad to bank through turns.
  • Hold Boost: Press the big glowing BOOST button for immense speed surges.
  • Drift: Hold the DRIFT button around tight hairpins to charge bonus boost!
  • Brake: Tap BRAKE to slow down for sharp chicanes.
  • Use Item: Tap the gold ITEM button to deploy collected power-ups.
